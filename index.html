01 · The oven

Into the fire

Hardwood burns against a dome of firebrick. The floor holds around 450 °C, so the base cooks from below while the flame rolls over the top.

  • Fuel Hardwood
  • Floor 430–480 °C
  • Dough 48 h cold rise
  • Bake About 90 s
02 · Ninety seconds

Leopard spots

The crust puffs and blisters into dark spots. A few seconds late and it burns, a few early and it stays pale, so the pizzaiolo turns it by hand the whole time.

  • Turns 3–4 per bake
  • Cheese Fior di latte
  • Tomato Crushed by hand

03 · Flour

It starts as snow

Wheat flour and water, kneaded smooth and left to rest, then rolled into wrappers thin enough to see the light through.

  • Flour Wheat
  • Dough Rested 30 min
  • Wrappers Rolled by hand
04 · The fold

Folded by hand

A spoon of filling in the centre, then the wrapper is pleated around it, one fold at a time, until it closes.

  • Filling Pork & chive or vegetable
  • Pleats 18 per dumpling
  • Hands Every single one
05 · Steam

Into the bamboo

Into lined bamboo baskets over boiling water until the wrappers turn glassy. Then the choice is yours.

  • Basket Bamboo, lined
  • Steam 8 min
  • Served In the basket
